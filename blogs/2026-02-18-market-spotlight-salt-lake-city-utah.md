August 25, 2026 by Hayley Levine Salt Lake City has earned the nickname "Silicon Slopes," not for the skiing, but for the tech scene climbing fast in the mountains around it. The city is ringed by the Wasatch Mountains, which came in handy when it hosted the 2002 Winter Olympics. It's set to host again in 2034.
