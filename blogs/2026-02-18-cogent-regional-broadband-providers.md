August 13, 2026 by Hayley Levine Regional and rural broadband providers are experts at serving their local community. They build fiber to farmhouses, wire main streets, and connect the schools, municipal buildings and healthcare locations that anchor a town. When a family turns on Netflix from their home in Fargo, North Dakota, they trust their local provider to bring high quality bandwidth right to the house.
