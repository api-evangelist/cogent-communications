April 8, 2026 by Hayley Levine This June, Vancouver is one of two Canadian cities hosting FIFA World Cup games, the first time Men's matches have been played there. But the World Cup has visited the city before - the 2015 Women's World Cup final between the United States and Japan. The first match of 2026 kicks off at BC Place on June 14 th and the seven matches hosted at BC Place will shine a light on the city like never before.
