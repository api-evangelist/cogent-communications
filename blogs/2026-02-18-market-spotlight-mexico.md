May 12, 2026 by Hayley Levine Mexico will be a host country for the World Cup for the second time in its history. The first time was in 1986 when Argentina defeated West Germany 3-2 in the final at Estadio Azteca. This time three cities will host matches, Mexico City, Guadalajara, and Monterrey.
